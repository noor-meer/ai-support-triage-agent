# TaskFlow — Support Knowledge Base

*Fictional SaaS product: a task/project management tool for small teams. Used as the knowledge base for the AI Support Agent's RAG upgrade.*

---

## Pricing & Plans

TaskFlow offers three plans:
- **Free Plan:** Up to 3 team members, 2 active projects, 100MB file storage. No credit card required.
- **Pro Plan:** $12 per user/month (billed monthly) or $10 per user/month (billed annually). Unlimited projects, 10GB file storage per user, priority email support.
- **Business Plan:** $22 per user/month (billed monthly) or $18 per user/month (billed annually). Unlimited everything, 50GB storage per user, dedicated account manager, SSO/SAML login, priority phone support.

## Free Trial

TaskFlow offers a **14-day free trial** of the Pro Plan — no credit card required to start. At the end of the trial, the account automatically reverts to the Free Plan unless the user upgrades. No charges happen automatically.

## Cancellation & Refund Policy

Users can cancel their subscription anytime from Settings → Billing → Cancel Subscription. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle — no partial refunds are issued for unused time within a billing period. However, if a user is charged in error or cancels within **7 days of a charge**, they are eligible for a full refund by contacting support.

## Integrations

TaskFlow currently integrates with: Slack, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Zoom, and GitHub. Integration setup is available under Settings → Integrations on the Pro and Business plans only (not available on the Free plan).

## Data Export & Account Deletion

Users can export all their project data as CSV or JSON anytime from Settings → Data Export. Account deletion is permanent and removes all data after a 30-day grace period, during which the account can be restored by contacting support.

## Team Member Limits

- Free Plan: 3 members max
- Pro Plan: unlimited members, billed per active member per month
- Business Plan: unlimited members, billed per active member per month, with volume discounts available for 50+ seats (contact sales)

## Support Hours

Email support is available 24/7 with typical response times of under 4 hours for Pro/Business customers, and under 24 hours for Free plan users. Phone support (Business plan only) is available Monday–Friday, 9 AM–6 PM EST.

## Common Technical Issues

- **Login issues:** Most login problems are resolved by clearing browser cache or resetting the password via the "Forgot Password" link. TaskFlow does not currently support login via personal Gmail/Google OAuth — only email/password or SSO (Business plan).
- **File upload failures:** Usually caused by exceeding the plan's storage limit or a single file exceeding 25MB (max file size on all plans).
- **Mobile app:** TaskFlow has iOS and Android apps, but currently does not support offline mode — an internet connection is required to sync changes.

## Security & Compliance

TaskFlow is SOC 2 Type II compliant and encrypts all data at rest and in transit. TaskFlow does not currently offer HIPAA compliance and is not recommended for storing protected health information.
